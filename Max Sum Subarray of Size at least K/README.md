### 📝 Problem Statement

Given an array arr[] and an integer k, find the maximum sum among all contiguous subarrays having a length greater than or equal to k.
Examples:


### Input:
 arr[] = [1, -2, 2, -3], k = 3


### Output:
 1


### Explanation:
 The sub-array of length at least 3 that produces greatest sum is [1, -2, 2]


### Input:
 arr[] = [1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1], k = 2


### Output:
 6

### Explanation:
 The sub-array of length at least 2 that produces greatest sum is [1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1]


### Input:
 arr[] = [-4, -2, 1, -3], k = 2


### Output:
 -1

### Explanation:
 The sub-array of length at least 2 that produces greatest sum is [-2, 1]
