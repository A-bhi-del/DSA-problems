### 📝 Problem Statement

Given an integer array arr[], find the maximum subarray sum by squaring at most one element.Examples:

### Input:
 arr[] =  [-1, -2, 3, -2, 3]

### Output:
 10

### Explanation:
 The subarray {3, -2, 3} with squaring -2 will give the maximum sum as 3 + 4 + 3 = 10.

### Input:
 arr[] =  [-4, -4, -2, 5, 2, -1]

### Output:
 27

### Explanation:
 The subarray {5, 2} with squaring 5 will give a maximum sum of 25 + 2 = 27.
