### 📝 Problem Statement

Given an integer array arr[]. In one operation, you can choose an index and increment its value by 1.Find the maximum possible frequency of any element after performing at most k operations.Examples:

### Input:
 arr[] = [2, 2, 4], k = 4


### Output:
 3


### Explanation:
 Apply two increment operations on index 0 and two operations on index 1 to make arr[]= [4, 4, 4]. Frequency of 4 is 3.


### Input:
 arr[] = [7, 7, 7, 7], k = 5


### Output:
 4


### Explanation:
 The frequency of 7 is already 4, so no operations are needed.
